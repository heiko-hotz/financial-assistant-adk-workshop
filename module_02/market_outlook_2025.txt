
JPMorgan Chase - Global Market Outlook 2025
PUBLIC DISTRIBUTION

1. Economic Forecast
We anticipate a "Soft Landing" for the US economy, with inflation stabilizing at 2.1%. 
The Federal Reserve is expected to cut rates by 50 bps in Q3.

2. Emerging Markets
India and Vietnam are projected to be the fastest-growing economies. 
We recommend increasing exposure to Southeast Asian manufacturing ETFS.

3. Commodities
Gold remains a recommended hedge against geopolitical instability.
Oil prices are expected to average $75/barrel due to increased supply from non-OPEC nations.

4. Crypto Assets
Institutional adoption of Bitcoin ETFs has stabilized volatility. 
We maintain a "Neutral" rating on crypto for conservative portfolios, but "Overweight" for high-risk growth funds.
